Fireside Fables Paintathon - 2026


A paintathon is a digital art event where teams of up to six people work together to create a fully fledged illustration in just 24 hours. Each paintathon starts with the reveal of a theme. This year’s being, "The student becomes the master". It's not only a test of artistic skill, but communication, teamwork, planning and scheduling through different timezones.

My team of 4 started the project with a 30 minute brainstorm session, where each one drew between one and four thumbnails. After that, the best ideas got polished a bit until we had a nice rough piece. Two of us worked on the designs of the characters inside the picture, one worked on the design of the environment while I planned the composition and a perspective grid. After the 6 hour mark, the project became a juggling of rendering, putting our progress together and sleeping. Once everything was in place, in the last 2 hours, a cohesion pass was made, and the image was sent for revision.

Chroma Corps 2026 Animation

Chroma Corps is an annual digital art event in which participants try to make an illustration per day given a certain prompt over the course of 30 days. This year, a team of 30 people got together to create an ending animation to celebrate said event. Given the time constraints, this turned out to be a monumental task, and a challenge of teamwork over the course of barely a week.

As part of this team, I worked in colorscripting the animation and filling in some standalone frames, such as the mono-colored band cards seen at the start.
